# Using Sax Tutor: every tab

A quick tour of what each part does and how to get the most from it. Everything is **written alto pitch** unless it says "sounds" or "concert". Use the online copy (https://sax.wo0.dev) anywhere; run `sax` on the Mac to also **save** progress.

| Tab | In one line |
|---|---|
| [Scales](#scales) | Circle of fifths, 21 scales, notation, fingerings, and hear them |
| [All notes](#all-notes) | A fingering chart for every written note |
| [Tunes](#tunes) | Your charts: play along, chords, transpose |
| [Backing](#backing) | A band to practise improvising over, in any key |
| [Reading](#reading) | Endless sight-reading, with an optional microphone check |
| [Quiz](#quiz) | Fingering flash-cards |
| [Ear](#ear) | Intervals, chords, phrases, tunes by ear |
| [Tuner](#tuner) | Tuner, long tones, intonation |
| [Record](#record) | Record, listen back, compare |
| [Progress](#progress) (Mac) | Everything saved, plus a summary for your tutor |
| [Tools](#tools-drone-and-metronome) | Drone and metronome, from any tab |

**Sound.** Tapping a note, a fingering card or a key plays it (the "Tap sounds" box and volume slider are in the top bar). **Play** buttons always play. The first sound needs a tap; on an iPhone, if nothing plays, turn the silent switch off.

## Scales
Pick a key on the circle and a scale. You get how it sounds, where it is used, songs, the notes for one octave and the whole horn (low B♭ to high F♯, grey = outside your range), and a fingering card for every note. **Play up and down** plays either line at the tempo you set, with the notes lighting up in time.

## All notes
Every written note B♭3–F♯6 with alternate fingerings (bis, 1+1, side B♭, side C…). Click a card to hear the note.

## Tunes
Pick a tune. **Tap a note** for its fingering (the first tap counts as a lookup on the Mac). **Tap a chord symbol** for the chord panel: its notes (guide tones marked), the arpeggio and fingerings, which scales fit (open one in Scales), and how the guide tones move to the next chord.

Above the chart:
- **Key**: shows the whole tune in any of 12 keys, with spelling and chord symbols following; notes that fall outside your range are greyed.
- **Guide-tone line** (under the chart): the 3rd or 7th of every chord, each the nearest to the last, on the staff, with a Play button.
- **Colour chord tones**: each melody note is coloured by its role over the chord (root, 3rd, 5th, 7th, 9th/11th/13th; black = not a chord tone).
- **Fade names I know** (Mac): names under notes get lighter as your quiz answers and lookups show you know them. Online there's a Fade slider.

**Play** (the playback bar):
- **Tempo** 40–220, **Feel** straight or swung, **Sound** reed or piano, **Count-in**, **Click**.
- **Loop bars** from/to, and **Speed up +4 every N loops** to build tempo gradually.
- **Silent** follows the notes and fingerings with no sound: quiet practice.
- **Play from selected note** starts from the note you tapped.
- **Band** (tunes with chord symbols): none, swing, ballad, bossa or straight eights. Untick **Melody** to hear only the band and play the tune yourself; **New take** gives a different bass line and comping.

## Backing
Pick a groove (12-bar blues, minor blues, ii–V–I, ii–V–i, turnaround, So What vamp) and a key; the chord changes show in written pitch and the sounding bar lights up. The playback bar works as on Tunes: tempo, swing, **band style**, count-in, **loop bars** and **speed up +4 every N loops**. The buttons under the title open the scales that fit, built on that key. Untick **Melody** (it has none anyway): you are the soloist.

## Reading
Pick a key and scale; you get a fresh exercise in your range (C4–D♯6). Play it first. Then:
- **Check fingering** (tap a note for its keys, counted as a lookup) or **Mark fumbled**. **Played it: save** records how it went (unmarked notes count as clean) and moves on.
- **Listen** (needs the microphone): the marker follows your playing, moves on when you hit the right note and marks a fumble when you hold a wrong one. It saves by itself at the end.
- **Key signature** prints the scale's signature instead of writing accidentals, when it has one.
- **Play** / **Silent** to hear or silently follow an exercise.
- Notes you fumble or look up appear more often in new exercises (Mac).

## Quiz
Rounds of 20. **Note → keys**: a note on the staff; press the keys on the diagram, then Check (Enter). Any real fingering counts, alternates included; a missing octave key is wrong because it is a different note. **Keys → note**: see a fingering, pick the note. **Mixed** alternates. Notes: your range, the whole horn, the current scale or any tune. Missed notes come back a few questions later; weak and often-looked-up notes are asked more. After a round: practise just the ones you missed.

## Ear
- **Intervals**: two notes (up, down or together); name the interval. A few to start; one more joins for each you learn (6+ answers at 80%).
- **Chord quality**: major, minor, dominant 7, minor 7, major 7, half-diminished, diminished 7, augmented.
- **Call and response**: a short phrase from your chosen scale; play it back on the horn, then **Reveal notation** and say whether you got it.
- **Tune by ear**: the first bars of one of your tunes with the notation hidden.

## Tuner
Press **Start listening** and allow the microphone (the audio is analysed in the browser, never recorded or uploaded).
- **Tuner**: the written note big, the concert note and Hz small, a ±50¢ gauge. **Casual ±12¢** or **Pro ±5¢**. **A4** 438–445 Hz. **Sensitivity** sets how loud you must play to register.
- **Fletcher mode** (off by default): the iOS app's insults, if you ask for them.
- **Long tones**: hold a note in tune; the timer restarts if you drift out (or stop) for 0.3 s. The bar fills over 30 s. Best per note is kept on the Mac.
- **Intonation**: average cents sharp or flat for each note as you play.

Use headphones if anything else is sounding, or the mic will hear it.

## Record
Record a take, listen back, rate tone / transitions / articulation / dynamics 1–5 with a line each, and tick **Compare** on two takes to hear them side by side. On the Mac takes are saved in `data/recordings/` with your answers; online they stay in the tab (use Download).

## Progress
Mac only. Tune milestones and tempo, lookups per note, quiz accuracy, reading fumbles, long tones, intonation, ear training, and **For your tutor**: a markdown summary of the last 7, 14 or 30 days to copy into the Claude tutor chat.

## Tools: drone and metronome
**Tools** in the top bar opens a panel that stays open and keeps running as you change tabs.
- **Drone**: choose a written note and octave (it shows what sounds); optional fifth. Play the note against it to hear your tuning. Same reedy sound as the iOS app.
- **Metronome**: tempo, tap tempo, beats per bar, quarters, eighths (optionally swung) or triplets, and a flashing beat. **Space** starts and stops it while the panel is open.

## On your phone
Open https://sax.wo0.dev in Safari, Share, **Add to Home Screen**. It opens full screen like an app and works offline after the first visit. The Tunes tab needs the sheet-music passphrase once on the device. Nothing is saved on the phone except what the browser remembers (your last tune, sound settings); progress is saved only by the Mac version.
